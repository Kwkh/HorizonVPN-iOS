# HORIZON App Icon · v2

使用内置 imagegen 生成。根据用户反馈，将第一版的粒子、光影与写实细节简化为黑底白色单线符号。

设计稿：`horizon-app-icon-v2.png`。地球弧线从墙沿后升起，少量错缝暗示砖墙；保留大面积留白。当前为设计预览，尚未替换 Xcode AppIcon。

## 完整生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Create ONE radically minimalist HORIZON iOS app icon, a finished opaque square artwork, pure black background, white linework only. The brand concept is looking beyond a wall at the world, but express it as an elegant simple symbolic mark, NOT a scene or an illustration.
Composition: centered, generous negative space, one clean thin white upper circular globe contour rising above a single horizontal horizon/wall line. The circle is partially hidden by that line, so only its upper approximately two-thirds is visible. Inside the circle use ONLY two restrained curved meridian strokes and one shallow curved latitude stroke to suggest a globe. Below the horizon line use just one short horizontal mortar line and two or three offset short vertical joints to suggest a tiny fragment of a brick wall. Keep all strokes geometrically coherent and well spaced. The wall portion forms a compact low band, not a large grid filling the canvas. The total symbol occupies about 62% of image width and 57% of image height. Clean monoline vector-like drawing with consistent moderately thin bright white strokes, perfectly smooth anti-aliased edges. Black brick faces blend completely into background. Symmetric, calm, minimal, precise, sophisticated, matches a native black-and-white line-based interface. The icon must read beautifully at 32px and 60px.
Absolutely NO particles, stippling, geographic continent detail, stars, photorealism, globe textures, lighting, shadows, glow, gradient, 3D rendering, metallic effects, text, letters, border, colored accents, extra symbols, or rounded-square tile outline. Do not draw a device or a mockup. Full square artwork with black corners; iOS supplies its own mask. One very simple white line emblem only.
```
