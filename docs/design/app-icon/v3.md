## 完整生成提示词

```text
Use case: logo-brand.
Design one sophisticated native iOS app icon for HORIZON, a monochrome cinematic architectural emblem. The first rejected design was a densely textured photographic particle Earth above a brick wall; the second rejected design was a generic flat latitude-longitude globe symbol. This design needs the middle ground: purposeful minimal linework with unmistakable spatial depth and authored composition, directly inspired by the actual HORIZON app, whose scene is a black solid wall outlined in silver-white and a globe rising behind it as the camera lifts above the wall.
Full square opaque pure black canvas. One coherent sculptural emblem, occupying approximately 72 percent of canvas width and 66 percent height, generous surrounding black space.
A quietly luminous Earth sphere rises behind a low wall. Globe positioned slightly above and to the right of center, approximately 52 percent of canvas width, with its lowest quarter hidden by the wall. A restrained thin white arc defines its outer silhouette, diminishing to charcoal on the far side, so it has depth without any photographic shading. Inside the sphere draw a small number of elegant clean fine silver-white geographic contour strokes: simplified but recognizably correct Europe and Africa, Mediterranean and Arabian peninsula. Use simple continuous contour lines and empty black land interiors, no detailed coastlines, no latitude-longitude grid, no wire mesh. Only 8 to 12 exceptionally subtle bright tiny points inside the continents may suggest the app's particles, but the contour composition must do the work. The globe is a designer's restrained line illustration of a 3D object, not an SF Symbol, not photorealistic.
Foreground: a compact fragment of black masonry with real thickness, viewed from just above its crest, forming a subtle perspective horizon below the sphere. Two staggered rows of long rectangular bricks, only about five main brick faces visible, each completely black with crisp moderate-thin white/silver edge strokes. Visible narrow top and right side planes use only a few lines to establish thickness. The wall extends toward the viewer on one side, a calm slight oblique perspective, not a perfectly flat grid. Draw only front-visible edges, no transparent back faces. Its clean long top edge passes in front of the sphere, clearly separating the near wall from the far world. The assembly must feel designed as one compact iconic mark, not a cropped screenshot of a huge wall. A quiet asymmetry and deliberate spacing create a memorable silhouette. Line brightness subtly distinguishes foreground and distance. Brightest stroke is the wall crest; globe remains clearly legible. Strict black/white/silver grayscale.
Mood: calm, cinematic, precise, minimal futuristic architecture, premium app identity. Use negative space as a key design material. Maintain legibility at 60 pixels and balanced shape under an iOS rounded icon mask.
No text or letters; no H monogram; no shields or locks; no photographic texture; no stone roughness; no realistic globe surface; no dense dots; no starfield; no rings encircling Earth; no latitude-longitude grid; no illustration scene props; no glows, bloom, rays, shadows, gradients, chrome or glass tile; no rounded square border; no device or mockup; no multiple icons or layout sheet. Square edge-to-edge black background; system will apply corner mask. One finished artwork only.
```
